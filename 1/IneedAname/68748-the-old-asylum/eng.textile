The old asylum

» 1. Prologue

“Flashlight, batteries, camera, notebook, snacks and drinks, cushion and duvet – check.”, Amber put everything in her backbag when she was sure she had everything she needed

“Are you really sure you want to do this, Amber?”

“You didn’t believe the stories, don’t you?”, she replied and looked with a serious face to her husband. This adventure could be the breakthrough from her career as a self employed journalist

“I don’t know, I have a bad feeling with this. You know, I don’t believe in this things too but how you explain the missing people?”

“Maybe they never was missed and it’s only a story like the rest? I don’t know, but I think in there will be lot of interesting documents. Also I think this campfire stories only exists because it was an asylum. When it would be something different I’m pretty sure no one would tell things like this”

“Yeah, maybe you’re right. But be careful and please leave when you feel uncomfortable or have a feeling like something is wrong”

“I promise!”, Amber give him a last kiss and went grinned to the door to leave her house “I will be back soon, can’t wait to see you again”

“See you! And watch out!”

“I will”, she give him one last smile before she sit herself in the car, put the address in the navigator to follow the instructions.

It was a 3 hours drive from her home, on her way she stopped at a gas station to buy something to eat and ordered a coffee. She hate long drives with the car, it always made her feel tired. A coffee always helped!

Amber continued her drive on the boring route, listening to her radio and keep the focus on the road

“You reached the goal”, the navigator computer voice said and Amber was happy, but somehow started to feel uncomfortable as she took a look to the visible old building where sign with “Enter on your own risk” greets her.

Ignoring the warning shield she leave her car, grab her stuff and open the small door to get on the property.

When she was a few steps away from the huge building fear overcome her, she don’t know why but within a few seconds she have much more respect from her plans then earlier.

It’s only an empty house with campfire stories. Nothing can go wrong here!

A last deep breath before the woman went on the small path that lead her to the front door. She was pretty sure it was locked, a try to open the door approved her thoughts.

I have to find another way in!

She looked from left to right, up and down. Her eye see a small window, it maybe was her key to enter the inside!

###

“I’m in”, she texted to her husband only to notice there was no signal.

Maybe I should check different places for getting signal later, just in case. But I need to make sure I didn’t hurt myself, no one will help me here.

She use her flashligt to light up the room, seemed like a small caretaker room, she figured out that there was a lot of brooms and tools to repair.

She try to put one the lights in this room, to her wonder the old light bulb worked well and she was able to take a look to the full room without wasting her flashlight batteries.

Tons of webs and spiders, yes, that’s how she would explain this room.

She fast leave the room because she was feared from this animals but well knowing that this wasn’t the last one she would see on her way.

After closing the door she stand in the middle of a long hallway, the first thing she tried was again to put on some light that worked for her too. She look around and the first thing she notice was the marked bathroom door.

Oh, a bathroom would be nice! I start to feel the coffee I drank earlier

But soon she open the door she turned back, it was disgusting! Everything was so dirty there!

When she was real she didn’t know what she should except, no one cared for this building over several years.

She just hoped she would be able to find a bathroom that wasn’t that dirty to use. The building was huge and she was sure that every floor had tons of the toilets, one have to be clean! At least she hope, otherwise she was forced to hold it until she was out, but for now she wasn’t worried-when she was lucky she could leave after a few hours anyways.

“I think I check out the doors on this hallway first”, she put out her camera and was sure this will turned out as a fully success!
